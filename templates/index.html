Who is doing the soldering

Nullbyte Labs is {{ site.author }} — a cybersecurity engineer who builds adversarial simulations for a living and conference badges for fun. The tutorials here are the notes taken while figuring something out, cleaned up so the next person spends their time on the interesting part instead of the setup.

Everything is aimed at people who are early but serious: you can follow a terminal, you own a soldering iron or want to, and you would rather understand the attack than copy the command. New tutorials land as a video and a write-up together.
